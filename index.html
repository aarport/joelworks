How it works

No pressure, no surprise invoices.

  1. Tell us what you're picturing

    Photos, a layout, something you saved months ago. Whatever you have collected is the right place to start.

  2. We walk the space

    Measurements, materials, what the room will and won't allow. You get a straight answer on what's possible before anyone quotes a price.

  3. A detailed quote

    Itemised, in writing, with a start date. Nothing begins until you say go.
