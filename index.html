How it works

No pressure, no surprise invoices.

  1. Tell us what you're picturing

    Photos, a layout, something you saved months ago. Whatever you have collected is the right place to start.

  2. We walk the space

    Measurements, materials, what the room will and won't allow. You get a straight answer on what's possible before anyone quotes a price.

  3. A detailed quote

    Itemised, in writing, with a start date. Nothing begins until you say go.

Meet Joel

Joel trained as a Renovations Technician at Niagara College and has worked in the trade since 2018. He started Joel Works part time in 2020 and went full time in 2021: kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and the tile work that ties them together, across Norfolk, Brant and Oxford.

Clients tend to arrive with something specific in mind. A layout, a finish, a picture they have been holding onto for a year. The job is building it, and the difference is usually in the details: where the tile lines land, how a corner is mitred, whether a pattern holds true right across a wall.
