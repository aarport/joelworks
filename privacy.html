Privacy

Joel Works is a one person renovation business in Simcoe, Ontario. The short version: the only information collected is what you choose to send when you get in touch, it goes straight to Joel, and it is used to answer you and quote your job. Nothing is sold and nothing is shared for marketing.

This site does not track you

There is no analytics, no advertising pixel and no third party script of any kind on this website. It sets no cookies. You can read every page without telling us anything about yourself.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any web host GitHub records basic request information such as your IP address in its own server logs. That is GitHub’s data, not ours, and Joel has no access to it.

What the contact form collects

If you fill in the form on the contact page, you are sending:

  • Your name and phone number, which are required so Joel can reply
  • Your email address, if you choose to add one
  • Your town, if you choose to add one
  • The type of job, from the dropdown
  • Whatever you write in the details box

Please only put in the details box what you would be comfortable saying over the phone. It is a quote enquiry, not a secure channel, so there is no need to include anything sensitive.

Where the form sends it

The form is handled by FormSubmit, an outside service that takes the submission and emails it to Joel. It passes through their systems on the way. Joel does not have an account with them and they are not given the information for any purpose other than delivering that one email.

If you would rather skip that step entirely, call or text (226) 931-5768 or email joel@joelworks.ca directly.

Calls, texts and email

When you call, text or email, that message sits in Joel’s phone or email account the same way any other message from a customer would. It is used to get back to you and to keep track of the work.

What it is used for

Answering you, preparing a quote, and doing the job if you go ahead. That is all. Your information is never sold, rented, or handed to anyone for advertising.

The one time it goes any further is when doing your job requires it, for example giving a countertop supplier the measurements and an address for a template appointment. Only what is needed gets passed on, and only for work you have asked for.

Photos of your job

Joel photographs finished work for the gallery on this site and for his Instagram and Facebook pages. Photos are of the work itself, never of people, and location data is stripped from every image before it is published. Nothing shown identifies whose house it is.

If you would prefer your job not be photographed or posted at all, say so at any point and that is the end of it, including after the fact.

How long it is kept

Enquiry emails stay in Joel’s email account so he can look back at what was discussed if you get in touch again, which customers often do years later. There is no automatic deletion schedule. If you want yours removed, ask and it will be deleted.

Your choices

You can ask Joel what he holds about you, ask him to correct it, or ask him to delete it. Email joel@joelworks.ca or call (226) 931-5768 and he will sort it out. There is no charge and you do not need to give a reason.

This is a small business, so the answer will come from Joel himself rather than a department.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, so what you type into the form is encrypted in transit. Beyond that, your information is protected the way anything on a working person’s phone and email account is: with a passcode, and by not being shared. No system is perfect, and it is worth knowing that rather than being promised otherwise.

Changes

If how any of this works changes, this page changes with it and the date below is updated.

Questions or complaints

Ask Joel first: joel@joelworks.ca. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, which oversees the federal privacy law that applies to businesses like this one, at priv.gc.ca.
