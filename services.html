What we do

Interior renovation across Norfolk, Brant and Oxford. Most jobs start with a room someone has been picturing for a year.

01

Kitchens

The room everyone ends up standing in. Built around how you actually cook and gather, not the layout that came with the house.

Get a kitchen quote

Flooring carried through from the rest of the house, and licensed plumbing and electrical trades coordinated for you.

02

Bathrooms

A room you are glad to show people. Custom showers, stone and tile set by hand, down to where the grout lines land.

Get a bathroom quote

Custom walk-in and low-step showers, benches and niches planned into the tile, and fully waterproofed shower systems. Joel is trained in Schluter’s installation method, which is what goes in behind the tile and is the part you never see again once a shower is finished.

03

Basements

A proper floor of the house rather than somewhere the kids get sent. Finished so it never reads as a basement.

Get a basement quote

Basement bathrooms and ensuites, rec rooms, offices and guest bedrooms, framing and insulation through to paint.

04

Tile work

The part of a room people run their hand over. Layout and alignment are decided before a single tile is cut.

Get a tile quote

Heated floor systems, curbless shower floors, feature walls and hearths.

05

Finish carpentry

The last ten percent, which is the part guests notice. Usually the difference between a good room and a finished one.

Get a carpentry quote

Panelled walls and wainscoting, crown, casing and baseboard, fitted closets, and hardwood, engineered or vinyl plank flooring.

What we don’t take on

To save you a phone call, here’s what to call someone else about:

  • Roofing
  • Exterior siding
  • Windows & doors
  • Plumbing
  • Electrical

Licensed plumbing and electrical work that’s part of a renovation gets handled by trades we bring in. For anything else on that list, ask anyway. We can usually point you to someone local who does it.
